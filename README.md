# SimpleQuest
Simple. Powerful. Direct.

SimpleQuest is a lightweight, no-nonsense quest management addon designed to streamline your gameplay. No bloated interfaces, no unnecessary distractions—just the essential tools to make your adventures smoother.

**🚀 Key Capabilities**

**📍 Zero-Clutter Quest Tracking**
Tired of a quest log filled with objectives from three zones ago? SimpleQuest manages your tracker for you.

Automatic Zone Tracking: The addon automatically adds quests to your tracker when you enter a zone and removes them when you leave.
Control your OCD! Don't let it control you!

**⚡ Enhanced Questing Speed**
Stop wasting time on repetitive clicks. SimpleQuest automates the tedious parts of questing so you can get back to the action.

Auto-Accept: Quickly accept new quests the moment they are offered.
Auto-Turn-In: Automatically completes quests at the correct NPCs as soon as you meet the requirements.
Tip: Holding Shift while interacting with an NPC will suppress auto-turn-in, giving you full manual control.

**🛠 Commands & Configuration**
Open the minimal settings panel using the following slash commands:

/sq
/simplequest

**Configuration Options**
Via the settings panel, you can toggle the following features:

Auto-Accept: Instantly accept quests from NPCs.
Auto-Turn-In: Automate the completion of quests.
Auto-Track Zone Quests: Keep your tracker focused on your current location.
Hide All Tracked: Instantly clear your tracker for a completely clean screen.

**Sorting Modes:**
  Recent: Sorts your quest list by the most recently accepted.
  Distance: Sorts your quest list by the physical distance to the objective.
